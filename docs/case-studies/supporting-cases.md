## How the summaries were produced

The values come from `docs/public/meta/case-study-summaries.json`, generated by `scripts/docs/extract_case_summaries.py`. The script reads only the two aligned posterior arrays and exports the 16th, 50th, and 84th percentiles plus their correlation.
